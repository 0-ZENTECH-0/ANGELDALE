FAQs

Common Questions

What is the age criterion for admission? +

Playgroup: 2–3 years | Nursery: 3–4 years | KG: 4–5 years. Age is calculated as of June 1st of the admission year.

What documents are required for admission? +

Birth certificate, address proof, parent's ID, passport-size photos, vaccination record, and previous school transfer certificate (if applicable).

Are mid-year admissions accepted? +

Yes, subject to seat availability. Please contact us directly for mid-year enquiries and we'll let you know the current status.

Do you offer a trial or orientation session? +

Yes! We invite families to a free orientation session where children can explore the school while parents meet our teachers. Contact us to schedule yours.
