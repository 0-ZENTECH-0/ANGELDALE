About Us

Where Every Child Feels at Home

At Angel Dale Pre-Primary School, we believe the earliest years of a child's life are the most precious. Our experienced educators create a nurturing space where children discover, create, and grow with confidence.

Our play-based curriculum is thoughtfully designed to develop social, emotional, cognitive, and physical skills — building the foundation your child needs to thrive.

Our Programs

Programs Designed for Every Stage

Thoughtfully crafted learning programs to support each child's unique development journey.

🧸
Ages 2 – 3 Years

Playgroup

Our Playgroup curriculum focuses on learning through play, exploration, and developing basic life skills rather than formal academics.

  • ✨ Developing language skills through stories and conversation.
  • ✨ Introducing early reading and writing through play.
  • ✨ Building basic math skills with numbers, shapes, and colors.
  • ✨ Improving motor skills through activities and movement.
  • ✨ Safe separation from parents with gentle care
Enroll in Playgroup 🧸
🌻
Ages 3 - 4 Years

Lower Nursery

Lower Nursery is where curiosity blossoms. Children develop language, creativity, and early academic concepts through play and guided exploration.

  • ✨ Language skills through stories, rhymes, and conversation.
  • ✨ Art, craft, and creative expression workshops
  • ✨ Structured play routines and indoor activities
  • ✨ Language development and vocabulary building
  • ✨ Daily life skills like hygiene and independence.
Enroll in Nursery 🌻
🌻
Ages 4 - 5 Years

Upper Nursery

Upper Nursery is a structured, activity-based program that strengthens early learning, confidence, and independence in a joyful environment.

  • ✨ Developing English skills with phonics, words, and simple sentences.
  • ✨ Building math skills like numbers, counting, and basic operations.
  • ✨ Enhancing awareness of surroundings, nature, and daily life.
  • ✨ Introducing basic knowledge of gadgets and computers.
  • ✨ Daily life skills like hygiene and independence.
