About Us

Where Every Child Feels at Home

At Angel Dale Pre-Primary School, we believe the earliest years of a child's life are the most precious. Our experienced educators create a nurturing space where children discover, create, and grow with confidence.

Our play-based curriculum is thoughtfully designed to develop social, emotional, cognitive, and physical skills — building the foundation your child needs to thrive.
