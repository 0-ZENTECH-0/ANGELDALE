What We Train

Our Teacher Training Curriculum

Every training module is carefully designed to equip our educators with the most current and effective early childhood practices.

🧠

Child Psychology & Development

Deep understanding of cognitive, emotional, social, and physical developmental stages from birth to age 6. Teachers learn to identify individual learning needs and respond with empathy.

  • ● Piaget's stages of development
  • ● Attachment theory & secure bonding
  • ● Identifying developmental delays
🎮

Activity-Based Learning

Practical skills to design and facilitate play-based learning experiences that embed curriculum goals naturally. Teachers become confident facilitators of creative, inquiry-led activities.

  • ● Designing play-based lesson plans
  • ● Sensory play techniques
  • ● Differentiated instruction strategies
🔒

Safety, First Aid & Emergency Care

All our staff are trained in basic first aid, emergency protocols, allergy management, and child safeguarding procedures to ensure the campus is always a safe space.

  • ● Certified First Aid training
  • ● Child safeguarding & POSH awareness
  • ● Allergy & medical emergency protocols
💛

Emotional Literacy & Classroom Management

Teachers learn to build emotionally safe classrooms where every child feels valued. Positive behavior guidance replaces punishment with understanding and compassionate redirection.

  • ● Positive behavior support strategies
  • ● Emotional literacy & self-regulation
  • ● Mindful classroom transitions
🤝

Parent Communication & Partnership

Teachers are coached in effective, empathetic communication with parents — sharing progress updates, handling concerns professionally, and building a strong home-school partnership.

  • ● Parent-teacher conference skills
  • ● Progress documentation & portfolios
  • ● Sensitive communication techniques
🌍

Inclusive Education & Special Needs Awareness

Our teachers are trained to recognize and support children with diverse learning needs, creating an inclusive environment where every child can participate and thrive.

  • ● Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
  • ● Early identification of learning differences
  • ● Adaptive activity modifications
